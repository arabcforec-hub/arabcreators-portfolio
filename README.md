## Who we are

Arab Creators is an engineering consultancy serving contractors, developers and private owners in Saudi Arabia and Egypt. The technical office in Cairo works directly on live Saudi projects, backed by more than ten years of site and project-management experience across government, industrial, port and private-sector work.

## Services

| Service | What the client receives |
|---|---|
| **Shop drawings** | Coordinated construction drawings for all disciplines: architectural, structural, electrical, plumbing, HVAC and fire-fighting |
| **Quantity take-off (QTO)** | Measured quantities per drawing and per floor, with a dimensions reference that cites every drawing and sheet used |
| **BOQ and cost estimating** | Priced bills of quantities built up from material, labour and equipment, with indirect costs and profit shown separately |
| **Architectural and interior design** | Concept to construction drawings for villas, offices, retail and hospitality |
| **Engineering and technical consultancy** | Design review, value engineering, contract and tender support (FIDIC, Saudi government tenders) |
| **Project execution** | Fit-out and finishing works delivered through the group's contracting arm |
| **Occupational health and safety systems** | Complete OHS systems for companies and projects (ISO 45001 framework) |
| **Rope access works** | Access solutions for inspection, coating and maintenance at height |

## Turnaround

A complete shop-drawing and QTO set for a typical project is issued within **24–48 hours** of receiving the approved design drawings.

## How we work

1. Client sends the design drawings and scope.
2. We confirm scope, deliverables and fee in writing.
3. Drawings and quantities are produced, checked internally and issued as native CAD / Excel files with PDF sets.
4. Revisions are tracked on a transmittal register until approval.
